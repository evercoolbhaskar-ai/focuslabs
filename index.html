01 · The name

Every business runs on a chain.

A chain is the path work takes from start to finish. In real estate it runs from an enquiry to a call back, a shortlist, a site visit and, finally, a booking.

Every link depends on the one before it. When a single link slips, everything after it slips too.

02 · How we work

Most companies spread thin. We go narrow.

  1. 01

    One chain.

    We pick a single workflow in a single industry, and say no to everything else, however tempting.

  2. 02

    Total focus.

    We learn the chain from the people who live inside it, in their words, until the product feels obvious to them.

  3. 03

    Solved for many.

    We don't build one-off projects. We build one product that solves the chain for a large base of customers.

  4. 04

    Then, the next.

    We move to the next chain only when this one is solved.

Focus isn't doing one thing first. It is doing only one thing, until it is done.

03 · Chain one

Real estate, and Secona.ai.

Our first chain belongs to real-estate channel partners: the brokers who bring buyers and homes together. Their day is portal enquiries, calls to return, properties to share and site visits to fix, often held together by memory and WhatsApp.

Secona.ai keeps that whole chain in one place, with Ona, an assistant that asks before it acts.
